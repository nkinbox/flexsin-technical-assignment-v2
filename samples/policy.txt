Remote Work Policy

Eligibility

Employees who have completed six months of service are eligible for remote
work. Contractors are not eligible under this policy.

Equipment Allowance

The company provides a one-time equipment allowance of 45000 rupees for a
desk, chair, and monitor. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days.

Core Hours

All remote employees must be available between 11:00 and 16:00 IST.
